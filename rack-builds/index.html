Rack questions

Which totes will fit?

The configurator shows the available 27-gallon tote brands. Choose the brand you plan to use so the frame is sized for it.

Can I add wheels or a work surface?

Choose swivel casters and an optional plywood or MDF top in the configurator. Request a custom quote for open shelves or a mixed layout.

Can I order more than one rack?

Add each freestanding rack to the same configuration to compare sizes and get one rack-and-accessories estimate.

Where do you deliver?

ToteWorks serves Austin and nearby addresses. Delivery is calculated from your street address and ZIP code. Choose preferred times when they’re available. Your builder will confirm the date.
