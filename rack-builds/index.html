Rack questions

Which totes will fit?

Choose your 27-gallon tote brand when you design your rack. I build your rack to fit the brand you pick.

Can I add wheels or a work surface?

Yes. Choose locking wheels and a plywood or MDF top when you design your rack. Ask me about open shelves or a different layout.

Can I order more than one rack?

Yes. Add each rack to the same order to compare sizes and see the price for all of them together.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is available around Leander. Enter your street address and ZIP code to see a delivery estimate. I’ll confirm your delivery date with you.
